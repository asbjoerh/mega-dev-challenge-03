The shared parser reads every date month first; day-first tenants get day and month swapped.
hint
booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field for day-first tenants; people type month first, the form reads it day first.
shift
The date moves by exactly one day, not a swap; the same meeting shows different days for people in different time zones.

How the reports were placed

  • One id, one report. The export pages overlap, so some reports appear twice. Each id counts once.
  • By tenant, not by intake tag. A report’s problem comes from its tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts), not from meta.intakeTag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the guess is often wrong.
  • Shifts, counted twice. The desk marked the reports where the date moved by one day instead of swapping (desk-notes.json). They are a different bug with their own ticket, and they stay counted under parser, where the desk files them by tenant.

Why it was merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject alone. All three problems produce the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”), so they landed in one ticket. A new decision record should replace 0001; this page does not change it.
